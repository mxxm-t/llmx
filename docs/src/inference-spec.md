# `src/inference/spec.hpp` - speculative decoding's acceptance, proposers and draft length

What speculative decoding adds above the model (docs/SPECULATIVE.md, section 3): the acceptance of a verify's rows, the proposers that draft, and the length of a draft. `generate` runs the round over them ([generate](inference-generate.md)); the model runtime owns the verify's history (`Model::mark`, `Model::retract`, [runtime](model-runtime.md)). Namespace `infer` for `accept`, `infer::spec` for the rest.

- `accept(rows, n_vocab, drafts, k, s, end, gen, rng, pick) -> Accepted`: the rows of a verify of `k` drafts, row i the logits after the last pick and the first i drafts, sampled in order with `infer::sample` exactly as the run without drafts samples, one call a row; `pick` takes each token as that run does and says whether the request goes on. It stops at the first pick that ends the request or differs from its draft, or after row `k`, and returns the rows sampled and the last pick, so the tokens, the draws and the end are those of the run without drafts whatever was drafted.
- `spec::Proposer`: `draft(seq, history, k, out)`, up to `k` tokens likely to follow `history`, `seq` being the history's sequence in the model, or null for the model's own as `generate` runs it, fewer or none where it has nothing; it drafts deterministically and never touches a request's generator, so a wrong draft costs time and never output.
  `draft_all(asks, n)` drafts several histories (`Proposer::Ask`, each naming its sequence), each as `draft` gives it alone, by default one at a time; the server asks it for every request a pass takes.
- `spec::Lookup`: prompt lookup, the tokens that followed the latest earlier occurrence of the history's last three tokens, else two, else one.
- `spec::Embedded`: the embedded drafter a model was loaded with (docs/SPECULATIVE.md, section 7), over that model: its `draft` asks `Model::draft` for the chain after the history's last token, the last pick, on the sequence given or the model's own ([history](model-history.md)), and its `draft_all` asks for every ask's chain in one batch of the model's; it keeps nothing of its own.
- `spec::Acceptance`: a request's one acceptance figure, the average of the drafts its verifies kept, each verify moving it an eighth of the way to what it kept, starting at 2; below half a draft a verify the request drafts nothing for its next 16 tokens, then verifies once more. `verified(kept, fed)` also counts by draft position the drafts the verifies fed and those they kept (`drafted()`, `kept()`), the one owner of acceptance for the round and the scheduler alike; `keeps(i, prior)` is the chance a verify keeps its draft at position i, its own share weighed with four verifies at `prior`'s, or one half where neither fed one.
- `spec::draft_length(draft_max, tokens_left, context_left, columns, acceptance)`: the drafts a request verifies next, the one rule for every caller: at most `draft_max`, one fewer than the tokens it may still generate and than the positions its context has left, since the verify feeds the last pick and every draft, none while it rests, and at most `columns`, the rows the pass's decode kernels read each weight once for that its other rows leave, past which a draft row costs a weight read of its own; `generate`, one request alone, gives `draft_max`, and the server the columns its pass leaves ([server](server.md)).
- `spec::PassCost`, `spec::PassTimes`: what a pass of generated rows costs, a base and a row, and a step of the drafter's chains, a base and a chain, as `PassTimes` measures them: lines fitted to the passes' rows against their milliseconds and to the chains against a step's, each measurement weighing 0.99 of the one after it, known while the rows they were fitted to spread by half a row; a server running one width long enough forgets the price and measures it again.
- `spec::draft_depths(cost, decoders, keeps, takes)`: the drafts each request of a pass verifies, the price section 3 names: at a depth K a request takes min(K, its cap) drafts where their expected kept drafts, the sum of its `keeps`, pay for their rows at the rate the pass gives without drafts, and the depth taken is the one whose tokens a millisecond, the decoders' and the kept drafts' over the pass's rows and K steps of the chains, are most, none where no depth beats the pass without drafts; rows that cost nothing give every request its cap, and so does a price not known, unless every pass measured had the rows the caps give (`PassCost::seen_rows`), when the pass drafts nothing, so passes of two widths are measured: on a split where both users drafted in every pass the price never learned otherwise.
- `spec::kMaxDrafts` (63): the most drafts a verify takes, 64 rows with the last pick, the most generated tokens a pass's kernels are held to give the bits of single steps for (`backend-vulkan`).
- `spec::Drafting`: a request's proposer, its most drafts, the tokens its history held before it generated, and its acceptance, which `generate` takes.
