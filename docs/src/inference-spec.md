# `src/inference/spec.hpp` - speculative decoding's acceptance, proposers and draft length

What speculative decoding adds above the model (docs/SPECULATIVE.md, section 3): the acceptance of a verify's rows, the proposers that draft, and the length of a draft. `generate` runs the round over them ([generate](inference-generate.md)); the model runtime owns the verify's history (`Model::mark`, `Model::retract`, [runtime](model-runtime.md)). Namespace `infer` for `accept`, `infer::spec` for the rest.

- `accept(rows, n_vocab, drafts, k, s, end, gen, rng, pick) -> Accepted`: the rows of a verify of `k` drafts, row i the logits after the last pick and the first i drafts, sampled in order with `infer::sample` exactly as the run without drafts samples, one call a row; `pick` takes each token as that run does and says whether the request goes on. It stops at the first pick that ends the request or differs from its draft, or after row `k`, and returns the rows sampled and the last pick, so the tokens, the draws and the end are those of the run without drafts whatever was drafted.
- `spec::Proposer`: `draft(seq, history, k, out)`, up to `k` tokens likely to follow `history`, `seq` being the history's sequence in the model, or null for the model's own as `generate` runs it, fewer or none where it has nothing; it drafts deterministically and never touches a request's generator, so a wrong draft costs time and never output.
  `draft_all(asks, n)` drafts several histories (`Proposer::Ask`, each naming its sequence), each as `draft` gives it alone, by default one at a time; the server asks it for every request a pass takes.
- `spec::Lookup`: prompt lookup, the tokens that followed the latest earlier occurrence of the history's last three tokens, else two, else one; an image's rows (`ImageRows`, which every proposer's `draft` and `Proposer::Ask` carry, and `Drafting::images`) are no tokens, so no occurrence or tail holds one and a draft stops where one begins.
  `Lookup::convincing(history, k, out)` is the match that stands in for a model's draft: the tokens after the latest earlier occurrence of the last `kConvincing` (8) tokens, an occurrence that reaches the history's end going on into its own draft, or none; it returns the match's length, the occurrence extended backwards in the same walk while it matches the history's end, at most `kLongestMatch` (32), or 0.
- `Proposer::after_lookup()`: whether a history's convincing continuation is drafted in the proposer's place where it has one; `Embedded` and `DraftModel`, which run a model, take it at construction, false unless the commands ask (`Drafts::attach`), so each can be run alone.
- `spec::Source`, `spec::Record`, `match_bucket`: where a draft comes from, the request's own history ahead of a proposer or the proposer, and a request's acceptances: the proposer's and the history's, one for each match bucket (`kMatchBuckets`, a match of 8 to 15, 16 to 31 and 32 or more tokens at the draft's start), with the source and bucket of its verify in flight (`drafting`, `at`); `verified` counts in that acceptance and counts the tokens the verify generated against every other's rest, so none's misses rest another; `tally` sums a source's buckets by position, for the commands' and the server's figures; `lookup_resting` is whether all three rest.
- `spec::lookup_first(record, draft_max, tokens_left, context_left, columns, history, out)`: a request's draft ahead of its proposer (docs/SPECULATIVE.md, section 8), `Lookup::convincing` up to the count `draft_length` allows, none where the acceptance of its match's bucket rests, and no walk at all where every bucket rests; it sets the record's source and bucket and returns the drafts, none leaving the proposer to be asked under its own acceptance.
- `spec::DraftModel`: a draft model (docs/SPECULATIVE.md, step 6), a model of its own sharing the target's tokenizer ([pair](inference-pair.md)), drafting greedily, ties to the lowest id, on its own history, which follows the one it is given: a draft takes that history back to what it shares with the one given (`Model::retract`), feeds the rest in one pass, as generated tokens through the decode kernels where it is no longer than a verify (`kMaxDrafts` + 1 rows) and through the prompt path otherwise, since a catch-up of one or two rows took 14 to 17 ms through the prompt path against 3 ms a decode step for Qwen3-0.6B on an MI50, keeps the state there on a model that keeps one (`Model::keep`), so the next draft goes back no further, then drafts a token a step. It drafts for the caller's one history, the model's own, and refuses a sequence of another model; it drafts nothing where the drafts would pass its context.
- `spec::Embedded`: the embedded drafter a model was loaded with (docs/SPECULATIVE.md, section 7), over that model: its `draft` asks `Model::draft` for the chain after the history's last token, the last pick, on the sequence given or the model's own ([history](model-history.md)), and its `draft_all` asks for every ask's chain in one batch of the model's, which `begin_all` begins instead for a caller that records it on the head stage's thread and ends it (`Model::draft_begin`); it keeps nothing of its own.
  A proposer says whether its drafts run on the model's devices (`on_device`) and only the head stage's (`on_head`), the one kind that has `begin_all`.
- `spec::Acceptance`: a request's one acceptance figure, the average of the drafts its verifies kept, each verify moving it an eighth of the way to what it kept, starting at 2; below half a draft a verify the request drafts nothing for its next 16 tokens, then verifies once more. `verified(kept, fed)` also counts by draft position the drafts the verifies fed and those they kept (`drafted()`, `kept()`), the one owner of acceptance for the round and the scheduler alike; `keeps(i, prior)` is the chance a verify keeps its drafts up to position i, the product of each position's chance given every draft before it was kept: that position's share over the verifies that reached it (`reached`, fed it after keeping every draft before it), weighed with four verifies at `prior`'s, which is weighed with four at the chance measured at the last position either reached, one half for the first draft and one where none was reached, so a verify that missed early says nothing of later positions, a position neither has reached goes on at the decay measured before it, and a record of no verifies keeps each draft at one half; each verify weighs 0.99 of the next in the counts the chances read, while `drafted()`, `kept()` and `reached()` count whole.
- `spec::draft_length(draft_max, tokens_left, context_left, columns, acceptance)`: the drafts a request verifies next, the one rule for every caller: at most `draft_max`, one fewer than the tokens it may still generate and than the positions its context has left, since the verify feeds the last pick and every draft, none while it rests, and at most `columns`, the rows the pass's decode kernels read each weight once for that its other rows leave, past which a draft row costs a weight read of its own; `generate`, one request alone, gives `draft_max`, and the server the columns its pass leaves ([server](server.md)).
- `spec::PassCost`, `spec::PassTimes`: what a pass of generated rows costs, a base and a row, and a step of the drafter's chains, a base and a chain, as `PassTimes` measures them: lines fitted to the passes' rows against their milliseconds and to the chains against a step's, each measurement weighing 0.99 of the one after it, known while the rows they were fitted to spread by half a row; a server running one width long enough forgets the price and measures it again. `PassCost::rows_ms` holds the server's price table's price of each width, which the scheduler fills, and `spec::pass_ms` prices a pass by it where it is set, past its last set width or before its first by that width's price on the line's slope, and by the line where none is set, so a step in the cost that a line smooths over, such as a decode kernel's widest build ending, is priced as measured; `PassTimes::reconcile` keeps the mean of the server's time of a decode pass past its slowest stage's table price, which `server_ms` adds, so the table's stage times and the line are in one unit. `draft_depths` keeps a request's drafts where their expected tokens pay for the pass time their rows add, `pass_ms` of the rows with them less without.
- `spec::draft_depths(cost, decoders, keeps, takes, chained)`, `chained` saying which requests' drafts a chain gives, the others' costing rows alone, each depth weighed with the chain and without it so a dear chain does not take the others' drafts with it: the drafts each request of a pass verifies, the price section 3 names: at a depth K a request takes min(K, its cap) drafts where their expected kept drafts, the sum of its `keeps`, pay for their rows at the rate the pass gives without drafts, and the depth taken is the one whose tokens a millisecond, the decoders' and the kept drafts' over the pass's rows and K steps of the chains, are most, none where no depth beats the pass without drafts; rows that cost nothing give every request its cap, and so does a price not known, unless every pass measured had the rows the caps give (`PassCost::seen_rows`), when the pass drafts nothing, so passes of two widths are measured: on a split where both users drafted in every pass the price never learned otherwise.
- `spec::kMaxDrafts` (63): the most drafts a verify takes, 64 rows with the last pick, the most generated tokens a pass's kernels are held to give the bits of single steps for (`backend-vulkan`).
- `spec::Drafting`: a request's proposer, its most drafts, the tokens its history held before it generated, and its record, which `generate` takes.
