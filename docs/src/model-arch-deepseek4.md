# `src/model/arch/deepseek4.hpp` - DeepSeek-V4.1 configuration and plan

The module in namespace `infer::deepseek4` reads a `deepseek41` GGUF file's configuration, holds it to the rules DeepSeek's model.py at e5decd76, of the 1bae0e10 checkpoint builds its caches by, and plans the fork layout's tensors ([DEEPSEEK4](../DEEPSEEK4.md), step 4).
`src/model/arch/registry.hpp` registers it as `deepseek41` (`open_v41`), declared dtype BF16, with no drafter pairing until step 13.
Its math runs on the CPU (step 7): the hyper-connection frame, the window, compressed and indexed attention, Engram and the MoE FFN; a device backend lacking one of its ops refuses the file at load naming the op, and `serve` runs it through the checkpoint slots its entries layers keep (step 11).

- **Layouts.** The reader tells the fork layout, which the 1017 files on the test machine use, by its key `engram.layer_ids` from the upstream converter's by `ple.layers`; the upstream layout is refused by that key ("unsupported metadata deepseek41.ple.layers") until step 16 reads it, a file holding both is refused naming both, and one with neither is refused as missing `engram.layer_ids`.
- `read_config(file, prefix, vocab)`: every key the math reads is read and required, but `attention.value_length`, which takes the key width, `hash_layer_count`, which takes 0, and the candidate keys, all absent or all present.
  A key the math holds to one value is refused by name at another: `attention.head_count_kv` 1, `attention.value_length` the key width, `expert_gating_func` 4 (the square root of softplus), `expert_shared_count` 1, `expert_weights_norm` true, `hash_layer_count` 0 (V4's hash routing) and `rope.scaling.type` `yarn`.
  `attention.compress_ratios` holds the blocks' ratios and may go on with zeros of any length, the drafter's stages, which the 1017 file's 74 keys do not count (43 entries for 40 blocks); `swiglu_clamp_exp` and `swiglu_clamp_shexp` hold a clamp a block, each carried per layer, and the math reads each layer's own, which model.py's one `swiglu_limit` gives every layer.
  Types and ranges are `metadata.hpp`'s ([metadata](model-arch-metadata.md)), the UINT64 Engram arrays through `metadata::uint64s` and the clamps through `metadata::reals`.
- `check_config(config)`: the rules whatever reader made the configuration, which a later HF-directory reader calls too.
  The source lists are ascending distinct blocks; a KV or index source has a ratio; a compressed layer has a KV source at or before it of its own ratio, whose compressed cache it reads (model.py:740-747 at e5decd76); every KV source is an index source, since only it owns index keys (model.py:500 at e5decd76), so a reader's selections always address its own source's keys; the candidate source is an index source, with a positive block size and count, and every later index source, which masks its scores with the candidates, shares its ratio (model.py:502-503 at e5decd76 and model.py:570-576 at e5decd76).
  Engram's arrays match its layers and heads (multipliers a layer for each n-gram order, primes and offsets for each order past the first and head, a table size a layer), each layer's offsets start at 0 and every head's rows lie inside its table, the pad id is inside the vocabulary and the token map covers it with ids below the compressed vocabulary, so a file is refused as it loads and never at its first pass.
  The widths fit an allocation, the rotary width is even and within the head and the indexer head, and the output groups divide the heads' width.
- `layer_table(config)`: each block's `Layer`: its ratio, whether it is a KV source, an index source, owns the index keys, is the candidate source or reads the candidates, runs Engram, and the KV source whose cache it reads.
- `engram_hash(config, i)`: Engram layer i's `blocks::NgramHash` ([blocks](model-arch-blocks.md)): its multipliers, offsets and primes, the token map into the compressed ids and the pad id filling before the start without cutting in text; the constructor holds each to `blocks::ngram_rows`' bounds, the int64 one among them.
- `DeepSeek41::plan(tensors)`: each layer's kind is the flags of its `Layer` (`Kind`), its mixer roles the attention's, its compressor's and indexer's as its place gives them and Engram's on its layers, its feed-forward roles the hyper-connection's, the router with its bias and the routed and shared experts.
  The sinks, the router's bias and the hyper-connection bases and scales are norm roles and the hyper-connection mixes F32 tables, as `role_fits` takes them; `engram_embed` is gathered, with a `Lookup` of its heads a token, and `engram_wkv` is two roles over runs of its output rows, the streams' keys (`engram_key`, its first hyper-connection count x embedding rows) and the value (`engram_value`, its last embedding rows).
  A tensor its layer's kind does not read is refused by name, every role's tensor must exist, and an Engram table must hold its `engram.num_embeddings` rows.
  Each layer's plan names the ops and modes its parts issue beyond the common set (`LayerPlan::ops`): the mixer's hyper-connection ops, `norm_rope_partial` with `rope_forms`, `model_format_qdq`, `model_format_pack`, `sparse_attention_sink` and `routed_experts` for the grouped output projection, `pool_write` on a compressed layer, `compress_pool` on a KV source, `indexer_scores` and `topk_rows` on an index source, `candidate_blocks` on the candidate source and `group_rms_norm` and `signed_sqrt_gate` on an Engram layer; the FFN's hyper-connection ops, `sqrt_softplus_routing`, `silu_mul_clamp` where its block has a clamp and the routed products; and the head's `hc_collapse_norm`.
  A device without one refuses the file at load naming the part and the op, and a stream destination without one of the FFN's keeps the layer's experts at home.
  The residual is `hyper_connection.count` streams of the embedding width, the embedding copied into each; `slot_widths` gives the frame's slots (`Slot`): the streams, the pre the attention leaves for the FFN and the FFN for the next block, the mixes, post, comb, the collapsed row and the head's gathered rows, then the attention's query latent, heads, window key, output, window ids and grouped rows, and the sublayer's output (`block_out`).
  After the position tables come the first block's one-hot pre (`first_pre`), which `embed` gathers for every row (model.py:1162-1166 at e5decd76), and the grouped projection's group ids as U32 bits (`groups`), which the attention gathers for every row, then the Engram norm tables; the ones table is kept whether or not a file has Engram layers.
  Every layer keeps `Cache::entries` (docs/DEEPSEEK4.md, section 4.3) with a headroom of the window: a slot holding its window ring of twice the window's rows of the head width, and on a ratio-2 KV source the open group's kv and score rows, ratio plus the window of each; a KV source pools a row a group of its ratio, the entry then its index key, head width plus indexer width; an Engram layer keeps `max_ngram_size - 1` preceding ids padded with the pad id.
  Each layer declares the widths of the cache type `native` (`LayerPlan::native`): a ring row is its 32-value FP8 blocks in whole words (`window_words`, 132 words at V4.1's 512), the open group stays F32 and is padded to whole ring rows so every slot starts on one, and a pooled row is the entry's FP4 blocks under E4M3 scales then the key's MXFP4 blocks, each in whole words (72 and 17 words on V4.1); under native the step is told so (`Step::native`), and the model is the same otherwise.
  The position tables are a cosine and a sine table a rope over the covered positions, half the rotary width a position, and the index scores slot holds a float a covered position at the least compress ratio (`position_slots`): the window layers' rope at `rope.freq_base` without YaRN and the compressed layers' YaRN rope at `attention.compress_rope_freq_base` (model.py:681-697 at e5decd76), then the ones the Engram key's norm reads and one stream norm table of the embedding width times the streams an Engram layer.
  `engram_q` and `engram_k` are `source` roles, read on the host only to fill that table.
- `rope_frequencies(dim, base, original_context, factor, beta_fast, beta_slow)`: each adjacent pair's angle at position 1 in double, with YaRN's ramp where `original_context` is above 0 (model.py:369-389 at e5decd76).
- `engram_norm_weight(q, k, out, n)`: `engram_q` times `engram_k` from BF16 views, or F32 ones holding BF16 values as the tiny file does, the stream norm weight of Engram's gate, exact in F32 wherever the product is a normal F32; another type or size is refused.
- `fill_tables(tables, sources)` fills the ropes, each angle in double and rounded once, the ones and each Engram layer's norm table from its two sources.
- `hc_mix(b, c, fn, scale, base, x, ...)`: a sublayer's mixes as an F32 product of its `hc_*_fn` table, then `hc_coeffs` over the streams with the norm epsilon, `hyper_connection.epsilon` and the Sinkhorn rounds.
- `embed` copies the token's row into every stream and the one-hot pre; `mixer` and `ffn` take their coefficients, collapse and norm their input with the pre the sublayer before left;
  an Engram layer's `mixer` first runs `engram` (model.py:350-365 and 1265-1266 at e5decd76): the layer's rows by `lookup_ids`, which calls `blocks::ngram_rows` with the layer's hash over each view's token history, or the rows a `WeightRows` read ahead, gathered, then `blocks::ngram_gate` with the `ones` table on the keys' norm, the layer's `engram_norms` table on the streams' and `ZeroSign::kept`, and the gated value added to every stream; the layer's place in `engram.layer_ids` comes from `Step::layer`;
  `ffn` runs the MoE (model.py:794-899 at e5decd76) through `blocks::moe_step` into `block_out`, cleared from the `zeros` table: `RouteRule::sqrt_softplus` with `exp_probs_b` as the bias and `expert_weights_scale` as the scale, renormalized, the routed SwiGLU clamped at the layer's `swiglu_clamp_exp` and the shared expert's at its `swiglu_clamp_shexp` (the layer from `Step::layer`), then `hc_expand`; the routed weights scale the down products, where model.py scales the SwiGLU before `w2`, the same math rounded once more or less;
  a layer's `mixer` runs `window_attention` (model.py:766-791 at e5decd76): the query latent, its norm and the heads, the window key normed, each turned on its last rope dims in adjacent pairs at the window rope, or the compressed YaRN rope on a compressed layer, the key stored in the window's FP8 (`model_format_qdq`), or under native packed straight into the ring, a pack a run of ring rows (`model_format_pack`), then `sparse_attention_sink` over each row's window of positions, oldest first, the output turned back, the grouped `attn_output_a` (`matmul_grouped`) and `attn_output_b` into `block_out`, which `hc_expand` adds back into the streams.
  The window's keys live in each sequence's state slot as a ring of twice the window's rows, position q at q mod 2 x window, read as rows of the layer's whole state buffer; a sequence that forks reads its source slot's ring copied into its own, and an entry's rows go into its ring at most a window at a time before they attend, so a row never overwrites a position a later row of the pass still reads, and rows of one chunk side by side attend in one call.
  A compressed layer runs `compressed` before it attends (model.py:729-764 at e5decd76): a KV source's compressor projects every row's kv and score in F32 into the open group's ring after the window's ring in its slot, ratio + window rows of each at position mod ratio + window, at most a window of rows at a time, and `compress_pool` pools each group the rows complete; each latent's index key (`indexer_compressor_kv`, its norm, the compressed rope and the FP4 under a power-of-two scale) and its entry (the compressed rope and the FP4 under an E4M3 scale), both turned at the group's first position, become one pooled row, entry then key, stored by `pool_write`; under native both are packed straight into the pooled rows at the row's width, the key only on a source that owns keys.
  An index source then turns the indexer's query at each row's position, weights its heads by `indexer.proj` scaled by ID^-1/2 heads^-1/2, scores the visible entries in place (`indexer_scores`) and keeps min(top-k, visible groups) by `topk_rows` in the `index_picks` slot, which its reader reads as it left them; the attention takes those entries after the window's rows (`sparse_attention_sink` with the views).
  The plan's `carried` slots cross with the residual at every handoff: both pres, the latest source's new pooled rows, the latest index source's picks a reader reuses and the candidate blocks; the first reader on a device after its source's (`Step::appends`) stores the carried pooled rows into its replica through `pool_write` before it attends, so a split gives one device's bits.
  `head` gathers the wanted rows' streams and pre, collapses and norms them with `output_norm`, then the logits at the pass's dtype.

The CTest `arch-deepseek4` holds every refusal to its text, the table, the plan and the footprint, and the suite's `deepseek4` component holds the names to the step 2 writer's files and layers 0 to 4 to the golden ([TESTS](../TESTS.md)).
