# `src/model/arch/dflash.hpp` - DFlash drafters beside a model

DFlash drafters (docs/SPECULATIVE.md, section 11) in namespace `infer::dflash`: a `dflash` sidecar file that drafts a block of tokens from a few target layers' rows, its checks against the target and its configuration.
DeepSeek-V4.1's DSpark rewrites its sidecar into its own blocks ([deepseek4](model-arch-deepseek4.md)); qwen35 keeps a DSpine, DFlash2 or plain DFlash sidecar (docs/STATUS.md, DSpine drafters for qwen35, DFlash2 drafters for qwen35 and DFlash drafters for qwen35) under a `dflash.` namespace and builds its drafter itself ([qwen35](model-arch-qwen35.md)).

- `sidecar(side)`: whether a file's `general.architecture` is `dflash`.
- `check_sidecar(target, side, p, own, name)`: the checks every sidecar passes, each `dflash.` key not in `own` equal to the target's key under `p`, and a `tokenizer.ggml.mask_token_id`, which it returns; DSpark's normalization calls it.
- `normalize_beside(target, side)`: the registry's `normalize_drafter` for qwen35 and qwen35moe ([registry](model-arch-registry.md)). A sidecar's geometry is its own; its width, context and norm epsilon must be the target's, its `target_layers` ascending distinct decoder layers, and a target that already carries a DFlash drafter refuses it. Its tensors are renamed under `dflash.` and its keys under the target's prefix and `dflash.`, with the mask token as `dflash.mask_token_id`, so pairing ([pair](inference-pair.md)) gives `DrafterKind::joined` and joins it to the target.
- `Kind`: what follows a sidecar's layers, a plain DFlash drafter's argmax of each draft's logits, DSpine's code bridge and refinement tail, or DFlash2's convolutions and selector.
- `Config` and `read_config(m, p)`: the joined configuration, its kind, layers, block, attention window and candidates a row, taps, heads, rope base, feed-forward width and mask token, each layer's causality and window (`layer_window(l)`), and a DFlash2 sidecar's `conv_kernel_size` (`conv_taps`), `conv_group_size` (`conv_group`) and `selector_rank`, which the others leave 0.
  The kind is DFlash2 with its three keys, DSpine with `attention.causal` true, and DFlash otherwise; `attention.causal` sets every layer, and without it a DFlash drafter's windowed layers attend causally and its others not, as z-lab's `dflash/model.py` gives a config without `is_causal`.
  DFlash2's keys on causal attention, a DFlash drafter with `selector_top_k` or another kind without it, a window pattern not one a layer, or a rope over part of a head, is refused by key.
