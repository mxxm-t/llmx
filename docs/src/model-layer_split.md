# `src/model/layer_split.hpp` - a model split by layers over devices

Which consecutive layers each device runs, fitted to what each device reports free (`docs/MULTI-DEVICE.md`, phase 1). The file knows no architecture: an architecture describes what it asks of memory as a `Footprint`, and the split works on those numbers alone.

- `Matrix`: one weight as a device would adopt it: quant type, row width, row count and bytes in the file.
- `Footprint`: each layer's matrices, the embedding table, the head's matrix (the embedding itself when `tied`) and its norm, one layer's cache for every position the budget allows, the tables every device that runs layers holds (RoPE), and one row of a pass's activations. `infer::footprint` in `arch_qwen.hpp` computes it for Qwen3.
- `DeviceBudget`: a device's name; the bytes it reports free (`Backend::memory_available`), absent when it cannot tell, which is not checked, while a reading of zero is a full device; whether its weights read the mapped file in place, as the CPU's do; and `resident`, what adopting a matrix keeps on it (`Backend::resident_bytes`: the Vulkan backend adds the padded copy it makes of an F32 matrix whose rows are a multiple of 256 floats).
- `split_layers(footprint, devices, rows, shares)`: consecutive layers per device in the order given. Every choice of the first and last device to run layers is tried, those two carrying the embedding and the head and at least one layer each; devices that copy weights share the layers as evenly as their budgets allow, filled level by level, and host devices take what the others cannot hold. The kept plan puts the fewest layers on host devices, then uses the most devices that copy, then loads the busiest of those least, so a host device that stops being needed once the endpoint weights move can take a layer back. `shares`, when given, is each device's proportion of the layers, rounded by largest remainder; the fit is still checked. A device must hold its layers' weights as it keeps them resident and their caches, the embedding and the head where they sit (a tied pair once on one device, the head's norm always), the tables, `rows` rows of activations (the CLI passes a prompt's ubatch plus a server's sequences), and where it copies weights a reserve of 256 MiB plus a twentieth of its budget for tile split partials and attention merge state. A model that does not fit throws.
- `LayerSplit`: per device its first layer, layer count and the weights, cache and other bytes it was fitted to hold, plus the embedding and output devices; `describe` renders it for `--verbose`. `placement_for` in `arch_qwen.hpp` turns it into the model's `Placement`.

`tests/placement.cpp` checks even shares, a device without room left out, a host device given nothing another device can hold and taking a layer back when endpoint weights leave the device short, tied weights counted once with the norm, a backend's resident copies counted, unknown and zero budgets kept apart, shares as proportions and their refusals, and that a model built from the fitted placement over two CPU backends gives the bytes of one.
