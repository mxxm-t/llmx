# `src/model/slicing.hpp` - how a prompt is cut into slices on a pipelined split

The one owner of a prompt's slicing on a split, for `Model::prefill` (the CLI and `bench`) and the server's scheduler alike (`server::next_slice`, [server](server.md)).

- `prompt_cut(start, rows, stages, lines, cap, backlog, increment_ms)` and `prompt_slice`, its first slice: the cut of the `rows` left from history position `start` on `stages` pipelined stages, slices at most `cap`, the ubatch the scratch was sized for.
  `lines(band)` gives each stage's `StageLine` at a depth band and `backlog` each stage's time until the slices in flight leave it.
  A `SliceCut` is a first slice and a ratio: each slice after the first is that ratio times the one before, at most the cap, the last the remainder; ratio 0 marks a cut chosen without prices, whose slices are chosen again one by one.
  With a line for every stage, it scores cuts by the stage recurrence F[s][i] = max(F[s-1][i], F[s][i-1]) + t_s(r_i), from the backlog and each slice at the band it starts in: first every equal cut, from the fewest slices up until a lower bound (each stage's backlog, its least fixed cost a slice and its least cost a row) passes the best, then first slices from half to twice the best equal cut's, `kSliceSteps` (16) steps a doubling, at each ratio of `kSliceRatios` (0.8 to 1.2), each cut given up once it ends no sooner than the best.
  The cut whose last slice leaves the last stage first wins, so it never ends after the fastest equal cut by the lines; a slower last stage grows the slices out of a smaller first one, and a slower first stage with a small fixed cost shrinks them toward the drain.
  Choosing only among equal cuts again for every slice left a cut about 4.5% behind the best found where one stage is far heavier, as on a 96-row prompt over stages of 1.0 and 0.25 ms a row or a 2048-row prompt whose last of four stages pays twice the fixed cost; one plan takes 0.03 ms for 2048 rows and 0.5 ms for 64k rows on the rig's host.
  With a finite `increment_ms` only the cuts whose next slice leaves the last stage at most that much later than the backlog does are taken, the fastest of them, and where none is, a slice of `kSliceFloor` rows; the scheduler passes `kMaxStepGapMs` while an arrival admitted at once, or an admitted prompt yet to read a slice with fewer rows left than the slice, would wait behind it (`server::slice_capped`), so such a request waits at most that much past the passes in flight, however long they are, and otherwise none, as `Model::prefill` passes none.
  Uncapped, `next_slice` takes the prompt's own cut alone and a ubatch beside other ready prompts, admitted prompts with rows left (docs/SERVER.md, The service rule).
  Without, the cut the scheduler took before any price: about a 2S-th of the rows, so every stage of a split has a slice to read, at least `kSliceFloor` (128) rows; one stage takes the cap.
  A slice that starts in a band without a line of its own takes that cut too, never a deeper band's price: a 16k prompt's warm-up cut gives only band 2 a line, its slices in bands 0 and 1 being all of one size, and those bands' slices stay whole ubatches; a band later in a priced cut without a line takes the line of the band below it.
  A slice's row class follows its stretch's extent, never its size, so every slicing gives the same bits (docs/SPECULATIVE.md, section 1).
- `pipeline_add` and `pipeline_run`: one step of that recurrence, each stage's finish time once one more slice, or a pass of given stage times, runs through the stages from a given one on; `Model::prefill` keeps the backlog of the slices it has planned with the first, and the server's `next_slice` that of the passes in flight from the stages they have not recorded, the prompt's own slices by its lines and every other pass at its formation price (`Slot::predicted`).
- `StageLine`, `LinePoint` and `fit_line`: a stage's time for a slice in a depth band as a line, a fixed cost and a cost a row; `fit_line` is the one line fitter, the least-squares line through measured points each weighted by its measures, a point the time of one slice or more, with its slices' count and their rows, none before two rows a slice are measured or where the line has no positive fixed cost and cost a row.
  A cell measured only by a few slow passes at one end, such as a load's first passes, moves it by its measures and not to itself.
  The server's lines come from its price table (`PriceTable::chunk_line`, `fit_line` over a class and band's cells weighted by their measures), each stage's by the prompt's row class at a slice's depth band, priced only where every stage has a line at the band the slice starts in and at the band of the middle of what is left to read, and the server says on stderr when prompt slices on a split turn from the cut without prices to priced ones or back.
- `SlicePeriods`: the prefill's own prices by the depth band a slice starts in, the band `prompt_cut` reads, one line a stage.
  `observe` takes a prefill's slices and each stage's busy time on each, the device time the slice held the stage's slowest device from its first operation after its waits on other devices to its last (`Backend::busy_ms`, [backends-backend](backends-backend.md)), so a stage's point is its own cost and never the wait for the stage before it; the first slice has no submission before it to start from and is no point.
  A stage's line in a band is `fit_line` over its last `kBandPoints` (256) points, about sixteen prefills of sixteen slices, so the line follows the present costs and planning stays bounded however many prompts a model runs.
  Busy times leave out what a slice pays between stages (hand-offs, submissions), so after each priced prefill its wall time less the recurrence's predicted finish, over how much that finish moves for one more ms of every fixed cost (the cells on its critical path), is a sample of the fixed cost they leave out; each of its bands adds the mean of its last `kCalibrationWindow` (4) samples to every stage's fixed cost, held within `kCalibrationShare` (0.5) of the least fixed cost, so a stalled prefill moves the prices for four prefills at most and no further than that share.
  `cut` plans a prompt as `prompt_cut`'s one cut and follows it, choosing again only after a slice it did not price or one that `stop` ended; without prices it takes the cut without prices, its second slice halved so one prefill's points tell a slice's fixed cost from its rows.
  `Model::prefill_slices` reports the slices (`bench` prints it after `pp`, `--verbose` after a prompt).
  The simulation that chose the constants (2048 rows, 2 to 8 stages, a heavy first, middle or light stage, 2% noise a slice, 5% of slices stalled 30 ms, handoffs of 1 and 5 ms, eight seeds of eight prefills) finds the plans within 0.25% of each other on average and equal at worst at windows 2, 4, 8 and unbounded and shares 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0, and ratios 0.6 to 1.5 no better than 0.8 to 1.2: learned lines reach 97.8 to 99.4% of the best cut found on average (89.8% at worst, on a light first stage), and the true lines 98.5 to 99.9%.
  The score is the plan's finish at the true costs, so a plan's robustness to stalls, which only perturb the measures, is not scored; the prompt stays in band 0 and the cost has no depth term.
- `depth_band` and `kBandTokens`: the depth band of rows at a history position, a doubling from 4096 tokens, which the server's price table keys by too (docs/SERVER.md, The price table).
