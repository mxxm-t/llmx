# `src/model/slicing.hpp` - how a prompt is cut into slices on a pipelined split

The one owner of a prompt's slicing on a split, for `Model::prefill` (the CLI and `bench`) and the server's scheduler alike (`server::next_slice`, [server](server.md)).

- `prompt_slice(start, rows, stages, lines, cap, backlog, increment_ms)`: the rows of the next slice when `rows` remain from history position `start` on `stages` pipelined stages, at most `cap`, the ubatch the scratch was sized for, chosen again for every slice.
  `lines(band)` gives each stage's `StageLine` at a depth band and `backlog` each stage's time until the slices in flight leave it.
  With a line for every stage, it scores each equal cut of the remaining rows by the stage recurrence F[s][i] = max(F[s-1][i], F[s][i-1]) + t_s(r_i), from the backlog and each slice at the band it starts in, and returns the next slice of the cut whose last slice leaves the last stage first.
  Even lines keep the slices whole to the end; where a later stage or a deeper band costs more and a slice's fixed cost is small, the last slices shrink, which shortens the drain.
  With a finite `increment_ms` only the cuts whose next slice leaves the last stage at most that much later than the backlog does are taken, the fastest of them, and where none is, a slice of `kSliceFloor` rows; the scheduler passes `kMaxStepGapMs` while an arrival admitted at once, or an admitted prompt yet to read a slice with fewer rows left than the slice, would wait behind it (`server::slice_capped`), so such a request waits at most that much past the passes in flight, however long they are, and otherwise none, as `Model::prefill` passes none.
  Uncapped, `next_slice` takes the prompt's own cut alone and a ubatch beside other ready prompts, admitted prompts with rows left (docs/SERVER.md, The service rule).
  Without, the cut the scheduler took before any price: about a 2S-th of the rows, so every stage of a split has a slice to read, at least `kSliceFloor` (128) rows; one stage takes the cap.
  A slice's row class follows its stretch's extent, never its size, so every slicing gives the same bits (docs/SPECULATIVE.md, section 1).
- `pipeline_add` and `pipeline_run`: one step of that recurrence, each stage's finish time once one more slice, or a pass of given stage times, runs through the stages from a given one on; `Model::prefill` keeps the backlog of the slices it has planned with the first, and the server's `next_slice` that of the passes in flight from the stages they have not recorded, the prompt's own slices by its lines and every other pass at its formation price (`Slot::predicted`).
- `StageLine`, `LinePoint` and `fit_line`: a stage's time for a slice as a line, fixed plus a cost a row and a cost a row per token of history at the slice's middle, which the server's lines leave at 0; `fit_line` is the one line fitter, the least-squares line through measured points each weighted by its measures, the fit without the depth term where the positions cannot tell it or it comes out negative, none before two row counts are measured or where the line has no positive fixed cost and cost a row.
  A cell measured only by a few slow passes at one end, such as a load's first passes, moves it by its measures and not to itself.
  The server's lines come from its price table (`PriceTable::chunk_line`, `fit_line` over a class and band's cells weighted by their measures), each stage's by the prompt's row class at a slice's depth band, priced only where every stage has a line at the band of the middle of what is left to read, and the server says on stderr when prompt slices on a split turn from the cut without prices to priced ones or back.
- `SlicePeriods`: the prefill's own prices by the depth band a slice starts in, the band `prompt_slice` reads; every slice after a prompt's first is one measure, its rows, its middle's history position and the last stage's interval since the slice before it, the last measure kept for each size and position.
  A band's line is `fit_line` over its measures, each one measure, so the depth spread inside a band is not read as row cost.
  The last stage's interval leaves out what a slice pays off the bottleneck (hand-offs, submissions, the fill), so after each priced prefill its wall time less the recurrence's predicted finish, spread over its slices, is added to each of its bands' fixed cost as a running mean.
  `prefill` takes the line, every stage at that period, so the first prompts take the cut without prices and later ones the measured cut, which `Model::prefill_slices` reports (`bench` prints it after `pp`, `--verbose` after a prompt).
- `depth_band` and `kBandTokens`: the depth band of rows at a history position, a doubling from 4096 tokens, which the server's price table keys by too (docs/SERVER.md, The price table).
